Taste · Downtown Wilmington

A good day starts
with something good.

Coffee and pastries, Latin-inspired flavors, and North Carolina seafood. Here are three downtown places to help you find your first table.

Coffee, bold flavors & the coast

Make room at the table.

A small starting list, rather than a ranking. Browse each venue's current menu to find your fit.

01 · Coffee & a pause

Bespoke

Start with coffee and pastries at this downtown café. Its menu also includes evening snacks and drinks, making it a useful stop at either end of your day.

202 Princess Street · Downtown

Pair it with: A wander through downtown before heading to the Riverwalk.

See Bespoke's menus & hours

02 · Latin-inspired flavors

Savorez

For a different side of downtown dining, try a restaurant inspired by Latin flavors and colorful cooking. Browse the menu before you decide what to order.

402 Chestnut Street · Downtown

Get a feel for it: Read the restaurant's listing with Wilmington Downtown, Inc.

Read Savorez's dining details

03 · A taste of the coast

Seabird

Seabird's seasonal approach puts North Carolina seafood and ingredients at the center of the menu. The restaurant occupies a historic building near the riverfront.

1 South Front Street · Downtown

Plan your evening: Check the latest dining hours and reservation options on the restaurant's website.
